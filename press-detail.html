MOCA Announces Digital Synthesis Lab

Released March 24, 2024
Press Release

Redefining the Institutional Narrative

Gandhinagar, Gujarat — MOCA Art India is proud to announce the commencement of its most significant physical expansion since its founding in 2018.

The new "Digital Synthesis Lab" will occupy the entire third floor of the West Wing, providing a dedicated 12,000 sq. ft. environment for resident artists to experiment with bio-feedback loops, generative AI, and spatial audio-visual mapping. This expansion is part of MOCA’s "India 2030" initiative, which aims to position Gandhinagar as a pivotal node in the global generative arts landscape.

“We are not just adding space; we are adding a new dimension to how art is conceived and consumed in the subcontinent,” says the Institutional Director. “The lab is designed as a sanctuary for interdisciplinary thinkers who often find themselves at the outskirts of traditional gallery systems.”

Bridging the Heritage Gap

A key focus of the new wing is the preservation of folk motifs through digital reconstruction. By using high-fidelity 3D scanning and neural upscaling, MOCA’s archival team will collaborate with digital artists to ensure that the "soul" of traditional Gujarati crafts survives and thrives in digital formats.

The construction phase is already 60% complete, with the modular labs expected to be functional for the Cohort 06 residency starting October 2024. The project has been made possible through a strategic partnership with global architectural firms focusing on carbon-neutral building practices.
