Facilities

Designed to foster creativity, exploration, and dialogue. MOCA Gandhinagar provides state-of-the-art spaces for artists and audiences alike.

Art Gallery Spaces

Thoughtfully designed gallery spaces that host solo, group, and curated exhibitions. Adaptable for diverse forms of artistic expression, from painting to mixed media.

Artist Residency Programme

National and international residency opportunities in a supportive environment. Artists are encouraged to develop new works and engage with audiences.

Film Screening & Seminar Room

A dedicated space for film screenings, artist talks, and lectures, supporting interdisciplinary engagement and dialogue around art and culture.

Art Camps & Workshop Area

Flexible area for educational programs and hands-on creative sessions for all age groups, encouraging community participation and learning.

Open Sculpture Courtyard

Designed for displaying sculptures and hosting outdoor art activities, creating a seamless connection between art, architecture, and nature.

Archival & Documentation

Committed to building a comprehensive archive of exhibitions and programs, contributing to long-term research and institutional memory.

The Museum of Contemporary Art Gandhinagar is a premier institution dedicated to the intersection of traditional heritage and future-forward artistic expression.
